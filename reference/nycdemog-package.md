# nycdemog: New York City Demographic Data from the Census

Tract- and block-level demographic data for the five counties of New
York City, derived from the US Census Bureau via 'tidycensus'. Provides
curated tibbles keyed by GEOID and thin wrapper functions for fetching
custom NYC slices on demand.
