# Package index

## Wrapper functions

Thin wrappers around tidycensus that hardcode NYC counties.

- [`nyc_counties()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_counties.md)
  : New York City county names
- [`nyc_county_fips()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_county_fips.md)
  : County FIPS codes for the five New York City counties
- [`nyc_pumas()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_pumas.md)
  : New York City Public Use Microdata Areas (PUMAs)
- [`get_nyc_acs()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/get_nyc_acs.md)
  : Get American Community Survey data for New York City
- [`get_nyc_decennial()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/get_nyc_decennial.md)
  : Get Decennial Census data for New York City
- [`load_nyc_variables()`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/load_nyc_variables.md)
  : Look up Census variables

## Datasets - Decennial Census 2020

County-, tract-, and block-level demographic tables from the 2020
Decennial Census.

- [`nyc_block_20_race_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_block_20_race_df.md)
  : New York City race and Hispanic origin by Census block, 2020
- [`nyc_block_20_adults_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_block_20_adults_df.md)
  : New York City adults and children by Census block, 2020
- [`nyc_tract_20_age_sex_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_tract_20_age_sex_df.md)
  : New York City sex and age by Census tract, 2020
- [`nyc_tract_20_household_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_tract_20_household_df.md)
  : New York City household type by Census tract, 2020
- [`nyc_county_20_race_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_county_20_race_df.md)
  : New York City race and Hispanic origin by county, 2020
- [`nyc_county_20_adults_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_county_20_adults_df.md)
  : New York City adults and children by county, 2020
- [`nyc_county_20_age_sex_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_county_20_age_sex_df.md)
  : New York City sex and age by county, 2020
- [`nyc_county_20_household_df`](https://kjhealy.github.io/nycdemog/reference/nyc_county_20_household_df.md)
  : New York City household type by county, 2020

5-year estimates.

  5-year
